Data and method

The calendar counts each 0.025° cell once per month and sensor. The historical series uses observed VIIRS from February 2012 onward. Earlier MODIS estimates appear when the district has at least 12 months with detections from both sensors in training and the chosen adjustment improves agreement in each of the three held-out years. Older estimates use the bundled boundary release and assume the relationship transfers back in time. A violet outline marks a value above all available preceding same-month values in the last five years, with a minimum of three. This is a descriptive signal for review.
