How this comparison works

Each sensor’s retained detections are compared with its own counts for the same month in up to five earlier years. At least three comparable years are required. January 2012 is excluded from the VIIRS baseline because the archive begins partway through that month. The review uses both sensors, even when the map shows one. The range describes historical detections. Observation coverage remains unverified.

Sources and counting rules

Historical NASA FIRMS India CSVs were filtered to Aqua MODIS and Suomi NPP VIIRS, with the same confidence and fire-type rules as the Sangrur comparison. Dates are in India Standard Time. Administrative boundaries are from geoBoundaries gbOpen and are applied consistently across years. Districts and borders reflect the source boundary release. Andhra Pradesh has 14 district features in this release. Zones are geographic groupings for this app. Detections inside a state but outside a matched district remain in state totals. Records outside all supplied state and union territory boundaries are excluded from the map totals. The totals use FIRMS country files for the selected archive year, grouped by local date. Late UTC detections that fall into the next calendar year are outside these totals. Zero means no retained detections in the selected records. Usable observation coverage remains unverified.
